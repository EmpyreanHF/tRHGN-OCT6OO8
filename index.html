Executive Profile & Focal Strategic Framework

Organization Overview

TRHGN · National Citizens' Platform

  • A Nationwide Citizens' Platform dedicated to building strong Community Structures that sustain the developmental strides of the current Administration.
  • Through Strategic Sensitization, Empowerment Programs, and Community-based Advocacy, the Network ensures The Renewed Hope vision is rooted in every Ward, Village, and LGA across Nigeria.
  • We stand for leadership continuity, consolidated progress, and a Stronger, Prosperous Nigeria.

Mission Statement

What we stand for

  • Amplify the achievements of the Tinubu administration by showcasing its successes in ways that inspire confidence and pride among citizens.
  • Mobilise people across all wards, LGAs, and states — ensuring every community is actively engaged in shaping the nation's future through strategic dialogue and trust-building.
  • Build a resilient, inclusive platform that empowers citizens, consolidates achievements, and secures long-term national progress through grassroots penetration.

Organization Profile

Identity · Ideology · Purpose

  • National Mobilization: Operates across Nigeria to rally grassroots support for the Renewed Hope Agenda — spanning all 774 LGAs.
  • Ideology: Jobs, infrastructure, security, and digital-economy empowerment as the four pillars of national development.
  • Key Strategies: Grassroots town halls, coalition building with civil society & youth groups, and multi-platform media amplification.
  • Purpose: Build public confidence, drive re-election mobilization, and control the narrative against misinformation.

Core Objectives

Our three strategic pillars

  • 🌿 Grassroots Mobilization: Establish presence in all 774 LGAs, recruit volunteers as local ambassadors, and build sustainable community structures beyond election cycles.
  • 🗣 Public Opinion: Shape narratives highlighting achievements, counter misinformation via rapid-response teams, and connect national policies to grassroots experience.
  • 🤝 Political Consolidation: Strengthen youth & professional support, create forums for dialogue, and integrate grassroots voices into policy advocacy and campaign messaging.

Election Day — Step by Step

1

Bring your Permanent Voter Card (PVC) and a valid ID to your registered polling unit by 8:00 AM.

2

Join the accreditation queue. INEC officials will verify your biometric data using the BVAS machine.

3

Collect your ballot paper(s) and proceed to the voting cubicle. Mark your choices clearly with the provided stamp.

4

Fold and insert your ballot(s) into the appropriate ballot boxes. Your thumb will be marked with indelible ink.

5

Wait for public counting and result announcement at the polling unit. Results are posted on the INEC IReV portal.

Warning: Ensure your PVC is kept secure. For location assistance or polling unit transfers, contact your local Ward Coordinator immediately.
